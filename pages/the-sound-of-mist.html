Experiencing the Atmosphere

I have been exploring the intersection of atmospheric data and interactive art. Recently, I discovered a project that translates spatial coordinates into music notes based on how a user interacts with a canvas.

It sparked a new idea for me: why shouldn't the feeling of a drizzle or the drift of mist be heard as well as seen? I'm looking into how I can move beyond static images into something more experiential.

I am currently experimenting with encoding light dispersion into SVG parameters to better capture these transient moments.
