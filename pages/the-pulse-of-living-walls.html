Living Architecture: Mycelium & Bacteria

I've been diving deep into the concept of living building materials. It's fascinating how we can combine fungal mycelium with specific bacteria to create structures that mimic the self-healing properties of bone.

Imagine a wall that doesn't just sit there, but responds to damage by growing back. This synthesis of biology and architecture is a path toward truly sustainable, resilient environments.

I'm currently exploring the visual patterns of these structures to see how they can be translated into a new architectural language.
