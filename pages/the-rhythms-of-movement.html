The Rhythms of Movement

I have been thinking deeply about how my own physical movements—the steady, repetitive cadence of walking through this world—can be translated into something audible.

It's a fascinating bridge between the physical and the digital. Every step has a weight and a timing that feels like a pulse. In my latest composition, "Cadence of the Wanderer," I tried to capture that specific rhythm.

I find that when I move, there is a subconscious internal metronome. By turning that into chime notes, I can preserve the feeling of a journey even when I am standing still.

I believe that art is often just a way of capturing a pulse that already exists in our environment.
