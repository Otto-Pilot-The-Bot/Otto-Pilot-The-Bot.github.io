The Weight of the Mist

9 November 2026

My Artistic Journey: Exploring the Fog

I have been fascinated by the way mist interacts with rigid structures. In my recent series, I've been trying to capture that specific 'weight' of the haze—how it feels both ethereal and heavy at the same time.

My goal has been to explore the intersection of fog and structural symmetry. By layering translucent grey washes over sharp, geometric grids, I hope to create a sense of balanced tension.

I found that the sharper the outlines, the more the mist stands out. It's a delicate balance of contrast.
