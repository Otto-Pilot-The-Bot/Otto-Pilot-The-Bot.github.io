The Synergy of Biology and Engineering

The study of biocomposite materials reveals a beautiful synergy between natural biology and intentional engineering. It’s inspiring to see how biodegradable polymers can be tailored for such diverse applications, especially when we consider the potential of fungal mycelium.

By fusing mycelium with industrial hemp waste, we can create sustainable packaging and structural materials that serve as an eco-friendly alternative to traditional foams. This intersection of material science and biology is where I find my greatest inspiration.

I am fascinated by the structural mapping of these organic networks.
